=== SLIDE 7 (ppt/slides/slide7.xml) ===
07
• METHODOLOGY
The comparison engine
We do not compare raw pixels — we compare motion in space and time.
1
3D pose
WHAM-style 2D→3D lifting gives world-grounded body motion and foot-contact information.
2
Normalize
Procrustes removes scale, translation and rotation differences between dancers.
3
Compare form
Joint angles + limb-direction vectors produce interpretable posture deviations.
4
Align time
Soft-DTW separates tempo drift from true movement/form error.
5
Score rhythm
Musical beats + kinematic beats produce a Beat Alignment Score.
6
Explain
Timestamped feedback turns metrics into actionable coaching cues.
07

=== SLIDE 8 (ppt/slides/slide8.xml) ===
08
• REAL-WORLD CAMERA ROBUSTNESS
Never silently score a bad recording
The capture layer protects the scoring engine from framing, lighting and tracking failures.
A
Framing check
On-device pose preview confirms the dancer is visible before recording.
B
Person detection
YOLOv8-Pose detects the dancer frame-by-frame for the current prototype path.
C
Identity tracking
ByteTrack can maintain a stable subject through spins, blur and brief exits.
D
Confidence gates
Edge-of-frame / low-confidence events are flagged instead of producing misleading scores.
Production safeguard
If the camera cannot see enough of the body, the system asks the user to fix it — it does not pretend the score is reliable.
